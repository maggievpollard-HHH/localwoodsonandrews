About Joe

Experienced enough for the complicated parts. Local enough to answer the phone.

Joe Pollard is a Charlotte-based business and operations leader who helps organizations understand their numbers, improve financial processes, and make better decisions. His approach is grounded in a simple idea: financial work should create more value than it costs.

Through Woodson Andrews, Joe provides hands-on bookkeeping, accounting, and fractional comptroller support tailored to small businesses, nonprofits, property owners, and local entrepreneurs. Clients work directly with Joe, not a rotating service team.

Joe studied at the University of Tulsa and brings years of leadership experience focused on operations, systems, and value creation.
