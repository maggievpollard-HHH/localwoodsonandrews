Frequently Asked Questions

Controller Services Charlotte NC FAQs

What is a fractional controller?

A fractional controller provides part-time senior oversight of the accounting function, with responsibility for close, reconciliations, reporting, controls, and financial accuracy.

How is a controller different from a CFO?

A controller focuses on reliable accounting, close, reconciliations, controls, and financial reporting. A CFO focuses more heavily on forecasting, planning, analysis, and strategic decisions.

Can you help if our accounting process is messy?

Yes. Accounting cleanup, process improvement, reconciliations, and controls are core parts of Woodson Andrews controller support.
