Before you meet

A little preparation goes a long way

  • Have the full document available, including any pages with the notarial certificate.
  • Ask the document recipient what type of notarization is required.
  • Confirm what identification and witnesses, if any, are required for your document.
  • Check whether everyone who needs to sign can attend the appointment.
  • Share your location and timing early so travel and availability can be confirmed.

Important to know

Notarization is not legal advice

A notary can perform an eligible notarial act but cannot advise you on the legal meaning of a document, select legal wording, or decide which notarization is right for your situation.

If you are unsure what your document requires, check with the organization receiving it or speak with an attorney. Confirm appointment availability, location, and any applicable fees with Joe before scheduling.
