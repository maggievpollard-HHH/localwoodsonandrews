HOW TO PUBLISH FREE ON NETLIFY
1. Create a free Netlify account.
2. Choose “Add new site” then “Deploy manually.”
3. Drag this entire folder into Netlify.
4. The intake form uses Netlify Forms and will begin collecting submissions after deployment.
5. In Netlify, connect woodsonandrews.com under Domain Management and follow the DNS prompts.
