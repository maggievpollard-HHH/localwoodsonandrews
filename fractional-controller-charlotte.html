Controller vs. Bookkeeper

When bookkeeping isn't enough

A bookkeeper is typically focused on recording transactions and keeping day-to-day financial information organized.

A Controller sits above that process. The Controller helps make sure accounts are reconciled, the close is completed consistently, financial statements are reliable, and accounting processes support the needs of the business.

If you need ongoing bookkeeping and accounting support, visit our Charlotte bookkeeping and accounting page .

Controller vs. CFO

Get the right level of financial help

Controller work is primarily focused on getting the financial foundation right: accurate books, a disciplined close, reconciliations, controls, and useful financial reporting.

CFO work moves further into the future, including cash-flow forecasting, budgeting, profitability analysis, scenario planning, and larger financial decisions.

Woodson Andrews can meet a business at either level and adjust the support as its needs change.

Frequently Asked Questions

Fractional Controller Charlotte NC FAQs

What does a fractional Controller do?

A fractional Controller provides experienced accounting and financial oversight on a part-time or outsourced basis. The work can include monthly close, reconciliations, financial reporting, accounting processes, controls, and oversight of bookkeeping.

When should a business consider fractional Controller support?

It can make sense when the business has outgrown basic bookkeeping, financial statements are not consistently reliable or timely, or the owner needs experienced financial oversight but cannot justify a full-time Controller.

Can Joe work with our existing bookkeeper?

Yes. Fractional Controller support can sit above an existing bookkeeper or accounting team, providing oversight, review, stronger close procedures, and management reporting without replacing people who are already working well in the business.

What if we need more than Controller support?

Businesses that need forecasting, budgeting, profitability analysis, scenario planning, or senior financial decision support may benefit from fractional CFO services as well.
