Charlotte Bookkeeping & Accounting FAQs

Do you provide bookkeeping services in Charlotte?

Does Joe work with businesses outside North Carolina?

Yes. Joe is based in Charlotte and works remotely with businesses across the U.S. If you need monthly accounting support or a one-time cleanup, tell Joe where your business is based and what you need help with so you can discuss the scope together.

What is the difference between bookkeeping and controller support?

Bookkeeping focuses on recording and organizing transactions. Controller support adds oversight, a consistent month-end close, account reconciliations, checks for accuracy, and financial reporting.

Can you help clean up accounting that has fallen behind?

Yes. Joe can help bring overdue books up to date through a one-time accounting cleanup project.

How much do bookkeeping and accounting services cost?

Pricing depends on the complexity of the books, transaction volume, reporting needs, and level of ongoing support. Joe discusses the scope with you based on what your business needs.

Do I work directly with Joe?

Yes. You work directly with Joe Pollard, CPA.

Can you work with our existing bookkeeper?

Yes. Joe can work alongside your bookkeeper or internal team on reconciliations, monthly close, accounting oversight, and reporting. The scope depends on where your current process needs support.

What should I share when asking about accounting support?

Describe your accounting system, how current your records are, who handles the books, and which reports or reconciliations are missing. Explain whether you need cleanup, ongoing accounting support, or oversight of an existing team.
