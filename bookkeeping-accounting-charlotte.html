Charlotte Bookkeeping & Accounting FAQs

Do you provide bookkeeping services in Charlotte?

Woodson Andrews provides bookkeeping oversight and hands-on accounting support for Charlotte businesses, including reconciliations, monthly close, financial statements, AP and AR oversight, accounting cleanup, and financial controls.

What is the difference between bookkeeping and controller support?

Bookkeeping focuses on recording and organizing transactions. Controller support adds oversight, month-end close discipline, reconciliations, controls, and dependable financial reporting.

Can you help clean up accounting that has fallen behind?

Yes. Accounting cleanup and organization are part of the controller and accounting services offered by Woodson Andrews.

How much do bookkeeping and accounting services cost?

Pricing depends on the complexity of the books, transaction volume, reporting needs, and level of ongoing support. Woodson Andrews tailors the scope to what the business actually needs rather than requiring a full-time accounting hire.
