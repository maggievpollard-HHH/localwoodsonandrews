Financial planning you can use

Connect the forecast to your next business decision.

CFO support turns your accounting information into a plan for what comes next. Depending on your needs, the work can include:

  • Cash visibility: a 13-week cash forecast to anticipate payroll, collections, and major payments.
  • A working budget: revenue and expense assumptions, with budget-to-actual reporting to explain changes.
  • Profitability analysis: a closer look at margins by product, client, or project where your records support it.
  • Decision scenarios: models showing how a hire, equipment purchase, or expansion could affect cash and earnings.
  • Management reporting: financial measures and reporting focused on the decisions you need to make.

Frequently Asked Questions

Fractional CFO Charlotte NC FAQs

What does a fractional CFO do?

A fractional CFO provides part-time senior financial leadership, including forecasting, cash-flow planning, management reporting, analysis, scenario planning, and decision support.

When should a Charlotte business hire a fractional CFO?

A business may benefit when financial complexity has outgrown basic bookkeeping or accounting but a full-time CFO is unnecessary or uneconomical.

Can fractional CFO support include controller work too?

Yes. Woodson Andrews can combine strategic CFO support with controller and accounting oversight when a business needs both a stronger accounting foundation and higher-level financial guidance.

Will I work directly with Joe?

Yes. Woodson Andrews is structured around direct access to Joe Pollard.

How is a fractional CFO different from a bookkeeper or controller?

A bookkeeper organizes transactions, and a controller oversees accounting accuracy, close, and reporting. A fractional CFO uses that financial information to help owners plan cash needs, evaluate profitability, and compare hiring or investment decisions. Woodson Andrews can combine these levels of support when needed.

What should I prepare before discussing CFO support?

Start with the business decision you need to make and the timing. Describe the financial reports you currently use, who handles your accounting, and where the numbers leave you uncertain. Joe can use that context to discuss whether forecasting, analysis, accounting support, or a broader CFO engagement fits.
