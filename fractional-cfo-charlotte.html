Frequently Asked Questions

Fractional CFO Charlotte NC FAQs

What does a fractional CFO do?

A fractional CFO provides part-time senior financial leadership, including forecasting, cash-flow planning, management reporting, analysis, scenario planning, and decision support.

When should a Charlotte business hire a fractional CFO?

A business may benefit when financial complexity has outgrown basic bookkeeping or accounting but a full-time CFO is unnecessary or uneconomical.

Can fractional CFO support include controller work too?

Yes. Woodson Andrews can combine strategic CFO support with controller and accounting oversight when a business needs both a stronger accounting foundation and higher-level financial guidance.
