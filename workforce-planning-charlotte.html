Frequently Asked Questions

Workforce Planning Charlotte NC FAQs

What is workforce planning?

Workforce planning determines the roles, capabilities, leadership, and staffing structure a business needs to execute its strategy.

How does workforce planning relate to financial planning?

Headcount is often one of a company's largest costs. Connecting workforce plans to budgets, forecasts, growth expectations, and operating priorities helps leaders make more informed decisions.

Can Joe help with organizational restructuring?

Yes. Joe can help leaders evaluate organizational structure, roles, capabilities, accountability, and workforce requirements during growth or change.

Will I work directly with Joe?
